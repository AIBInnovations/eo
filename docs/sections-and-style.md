Every traveller should ideally carry: 2 thermal sets, waterproof insulated jacket/parka, waterproof trousers, fleece/wool mid-layer, 2–3 sweaters/knits, waterproof boots, wool socks, gloves, beanie, neck warmer, sunglasses, swimwear and a compact day backpack.

For snowmobiling/glacier day, don't rely on fashion winterwear—the priority should be warm + waterproof + windproof. The itinerary does mention that thermal overalls, gloves and helmets are provided for snowmobiling, but members should still arrive properly layered underneath.

For the Day 4 breakouts, I would issue separate guidance once members choose between Silfra snorkelling, Lava Tunnel, Horse Riding and ATV, because their footwear and layering requirements will differ.
